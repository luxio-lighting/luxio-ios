Control your Luxio LED devices from one simple app. Choose a solid color, create a gradient, or bring your strip to life with an animation.

• Adjust power and brightness for each device.
• Pick colors and supported warm, cool or dedicated white channels.
• Create gradients with multiple color points.
• Play animations and customize their available parameters.
• Save colors, gradients and animations to your device.
• Find nearby devices, configure Wi-Fi and access Matter pairing details on supported hardware.
• Try the built-in Simulator without owning a Luxio device.

Physical lighting control requires compatible Luxio hardware. Available controls depend on the device, LED strip and firmware. Devices must be reachable locally over Wi-Fi or supported Bluetooth connections.
