Luxio now makes more room for your devices on iPad and Macs with Apple silicon.

• Browse your devices in a sidebar while controlling the selected Luxio alongside it.
• Switch between devices without returning to the home screen.
• Use the layout in portrait or landscape.
• The color and white-temperature switch is now centered below the wheel in Color and Gradient.
• On Mac, use the refresh button or Command-R to search for devices.
